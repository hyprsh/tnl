# The Edge may declare its Zones and Outposts, and keeps them in state.json

The Edge's settings can list its Zones, one `TNL_ZONE_<LABEL>` per Zone holding the arguments of `tnl zone add`, and its Outposts, one `TNL_OUTPOST_<LABEL>` per Outpost ([ADR 0010](0010-declared-outposts-connect-with-a-durable-credential.md)). As with an Outpost's Declaration ([ADR 0009](0009-outposts-may-declare-their-routes.md)), while any line of a kind is set, the declared lines are the Edge's whole set of that kind: `zone add`, `edit` and `rm`, or `outpost add`, `rekey` and `rm`, are refused, and the first start replaces what the CLI made. Declared items are saved in `state.json`, marked as declared, and applied at each start as a diff by name in one locked change, so `state.json` stays the one file the Edge writes, checks whole and backs up ([ADR 0006](0006-edge-state-is-one-file-the-edge-writes.md)). A wildcard Zone's token comes from `--token-file` or `--token` on its line, and is still never a CLI argument. Removing a line removes its item, but never loses a Route that can't come back by itself: if a CLI-made Route would end up outside every Zone, the Edge exits 78 instead. When no line of a kind is set, the declared items of that kind stay and go back to the CLI. This differs from ADR 0009, where an empty Declaration removes its Routes, because an Edge started once without its environment, from a forgotten `.env` or by hand, would otherwise wipe every Zone, Outpost and Route. A remote check that fails at start, such as a wildcard token that can't read its DNS zone, is logged and retried rather than refusing to start, so a provider's outage can't take every Hostname down. Routes stay declared only on Outposts, and the Edge refuses `TNL_ROUTE_*`, so each Outpost keeps one source for its Routes. We rejected mixing declared and CLI items of one kind, which needs rules for name clashes and nesting and gives two places to look. We also rejected merging the lines in memory only, which would leave `state.json` impossible to check on its own and backups with Routes but no Zones. The costs: removing the last line of a kind hands its item to the CLI instead of removing it, so the docs say to follow with `zone rm` or `outpost rm`. Secrets may sit in variables, where `docker inspect` shows them. Compose files depend on the syntax, so it can change only at a major.

See [Declaring the Edge's Zones and Outposts in its settings](https://github.com/hyprsh/tnl/issues/28).
